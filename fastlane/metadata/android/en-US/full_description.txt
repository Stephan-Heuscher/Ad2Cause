**Small actions. Big impact — watch short ads to support causes you care about.**

Ad2Cause makes giving easy and accessible for everyone. By simply watching short rewarded advertisements on your phone, you generate real funds directed towards good causes, charities, and community projects of your choice.

### 🌟 How It Works
1. **Choose a Cause**: Browse a curated selection of worthy causes across education, environment, health, and humanitarian aid.
2. **Watch Short Ads**: Watch standard video ads or engage with interactive ads whenever you have a free moment.
3. **Generate Real Impact**: Every completed ad earns points and funding allocated directly to your chosen cause.
4. **Track Your Contribution**: View detailed statistics and milestones of your total impact over time.

### ✨ Key Features
- **Curated & Custom Causes**: Support featured initiatives or request new causes you are passionate about.
- **Dark Mode Support**: Full dark theme support for comfortable viewing anytime.
- **Guided Onboarding**: Easy walkthrough to get you started in seconds.
- **Secure Verification**: Ad rewards are securely verified to ensure every view counts towards your cause.
- **100% Free**: No donations required from your wallet—only your attention.

*Together we can make a difference, one view at a time!*
